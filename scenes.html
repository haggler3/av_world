OpenUSD export · open map data

Long-tail scenes, set in the real city

Each clip is one simulated run. On the left is av_world's own view; on the right is the same run exported to an OpenUSD stage for NVIDIA Omniverse, with buildings at their mapped heights, trees, lamps and signals from Overture and OpenStreetMap. The frames, vehicles and signal timing are the same on both sides.

Made by one command

av-world export-nvidia out --area new_york --scenario black_ice writes the USD stage, a camera-view control video, and this side-by-side clip.

Crashes found automatically

--find-crash 600 runs 8 copies of the city until an AV crashes, then replays that copy with recording on and follows the AV into the crash. Otherwise the clip follows the AV with the most driving. Frames between simulator steps are interpolated, so clips play at 20 fps.

What it is not

The layout is real but untextured: plain facades, one tree model, no parked cars. The photoreal pass belongs to Omniverse or a world model conditioned on the stage. Details
