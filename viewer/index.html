      What you are looking at

      Every vehicle in the area is simulated together at 0.2 s steps: car following, lane changes and zipper merges, right of way at every conflict point inside a junction, signals and stop signs, pedestrians on crosswalks, buses at stops, double-parked delivery vans and breakdowns. Human drivers carry the risky behaviour calibrated to published crash data. Blue cars are AVs.

      Each AV carries a control barrier function. The band ahead of it is the zone it must keep clear: its stopping distance plus a margin, growing with speed. The colour is the barrier value h, the room left between that zone and the nearest obstacle: green, more than 5 m of slack; amber, tight; red, inside the barrier. When the filter steps in, the AV gets an orange outline (braking), a red bar (held at a junction entry it could not clear in time) or purple side bars (a merge blocked).
